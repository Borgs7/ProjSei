About Us

Making seismic hazard legible.

SeiScan_PH is a DOST-funded research project under the “Paleoseismology and Hazard Assessment of Seismogenic Sources in Northern Luzon, Philippines” program and the MECO–TECO Joint Research Project initiative. The project focuses on mapping and characterizing active and previously unmapped faults in the Batangas–Mindoro–Marinduque region and adjacent offshore areas, including the Verde Passage Fault, Sibuyan Sea Fault, and related fault structures, using seismic imaging and integrated geophysical datasets.

Learn more about the project →

What the platform does

  • Calculates ground-shaking intensity (MMI) at any point using the Wald et al. (1999) relationship.
  • Maps PHIVOLCS active faults and returns the nearest fault to any location.
  • Grows scenario by scenario, with bathymetry and terrain for the offshore picture.
